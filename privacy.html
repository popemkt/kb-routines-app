Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

What kb routines accesses

With your permission, kb routines reads and writes events in Google Calendar: the calendars it creates for kb (named "kb routines") and the events kb syncs into them. It uses the Google Calendar API only to keep those calendars in sync with the calendars you choose in kb.

Where your data goes

kb runs on your own computer or server. Calendar data and the access tokens Google issues are stored only there. Tokens are kept encrypted on that machine (the macOS Keychain, or an encrypted file on Linux). Nothing is sent to the author of kb or to any third party. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

Sharing

kb routines does not sell, share or transfer your Google data. kb routines' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Remove on kb's calendar sync page to revoke access and erase the stored tokens. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
